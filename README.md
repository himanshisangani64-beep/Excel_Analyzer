## 📌 Project Overview

This project uses **Microsoft Excel** to analyze customer purchases, sales, profit, discounts, product categories, regions, and monthly performance.

The workbook combines **Excel formulas, conditional formatting, What-If Analysis, Analysis ToolPak, PivotTables, and charts** to transform sales data into meaningful business insights.

An interactive dashboard is created to provide a clear overview of key sales and profit performance.

## 📚 Analysis & Excel Practice

### 1. 👥 Customer Analysis

* Identified the **Top 10 customers** based on total purchase.
* Applied conditional formatting to highlight high-value customers.
* Used `INDEX` and `MATCH` for customer lookup and ranking analysis.
* Added a region selector to analyze customer purchases by region.

### 2. 💰 Discount What-If Analysis

* Used **Excel What-If Analysis / Data Tables** to evaluate different discount scenarios.
* Compared how changes in discount percentage affect calculated price and profit.
* Tested multiple discount assumptions such as **5%, 10%, 15%, and 20%**.

### 3. 📈 Sales & Profit Regression

* Used the **Excel Analysis ToolPak** to perform linear regression.
* Analyzed the relationship between **Sales and Profit**.
* The regression analysis produced an **R-squared value of approximately 0.7012** based on 30 observations.
* This indicates a positive relationship between Sales and Profit in the dataset.

### 4. 📊 Descriptive Statistics

Used the **Analysis ToolPak** to analyze:

* Sales
* Quantity
* Profit
* Total Purchase

The analysis includes:

* Mean
* Median
* Standard Deviation
* Variance
* Minimum
* Maximum
* Range
* Skewness
* Kurtosis
* Sum
* Count

### 5. 📅 Monthly Sales Analysis

* Summarized sales performance by month.
* Compared monthly sales with the overall average.
* Added **up/down indicators** to highlight performance changes.
* Compared monthly sales and profit.
* Used formulas and conditional formatting to make monthly trends easier to interpret.

### 6. 🌎 Regional & Product Analysis

* Created PivotTables to analyze sales by region.
* Compared sales and profit across different regions.
* Analyzed product category performance.
* Summarized discount distribution by product category.
* Used charts to make regional and category comparisons easier to understand.

### 7. 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

Created multiple charts to present the analysis clearly:

* **Bar Chart** — Category / Regional comparison
* **Line Chart** — Monthly sales and profit trends
* **Pie Chart** — Category / Regional distribution
* KPI cards for important business metrics

The dashboard is designed with a **clean and simple layout** for easy interpretation.

### 8. 🕒 Timestamp

* Added a timestamp using Excel's `NOW()` function.
* The timestamp provides the current date and time when the workbook is recalculated.
